Real-data PTB-XL results.

The evaluation used 300 NORM training records,
100 NORM validation records, 200 NORM test
records, and 200 non-NORM test records. The maximum observed
Walsh/time-domain full-covariance score discrepancy was
1.097e-11. The maximum exact
decomposition errors were 1.364e-12
for the full-covariance Walsh model and
3.411e-13 for the block
Walsh model. These values confirm the operator identities on real PTB-XL
windows.

Using q95 record-level aggregation, the best observed model in this run was
walsh_block, with ROC-AUC=0.4616 and AUPRC=0.4853. The full
ablation table is saved in tables/article_ablation_table_q95.csv and
tables/article_ablation_table_q95.tex.
